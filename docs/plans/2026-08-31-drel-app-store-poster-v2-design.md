# Drel App Store Poster V2 Design

## Goal

Create one coherent App Store screenshot story for Drel 1.0 in both Simplified
Chinese and English. Every poster must show the real shipped Drel interface as
the primary evidence; generated artwork is limited to a quiet brand backdrop.

## Narrative

1. **Send** — establish the personal push address and immediate test-send flow.
2. **Receive** — show the Inbox with realistic incoming notifications and a
   clear recent-message history.
3. **Explore** — show the developer-oriented push examples and their copyable
   URL parameters.

## Visual Direction

- Use the Drel blue-to-violet palette over a pale lavender background.
- Enlarge the real app capture and reduce unused space compared with V1.
- Keep typography short, high-contrast, and localized rather than bilingual on
  the same canvas.
- Keep a consistent top brand line, headline position, screenshot radius, and
  baseline across all six images.
- Do not show Bridge, gateway, PWA download, cache-debug, or other functionality
  outside the Drel 1.0 Store scope.

## Localized Copy

| Frame | Simplified Chinese | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Send | 一条链接，立即推送 | One link. Instant push. |
| Receive | 消息清晰收纳，随时回看 | Every message, easy to revisit. |
| Explore | 丰富推送示例，复制即可使用 | Push examples, ready to copy. |

Supporting copy may clarify HTTPS destinations, custom avatars and grouping,
and the available Markdown, OTP, QR, image, and sound examples. It must remain
subordinate to the headline and actual interface.

## Deliverables

- Three opaque 1242 × 2688 PNG screenshots for Simplified Chinese.
- Three matching opaque 1242 × 2688 PNG screenshots for English.
- A contact sheet for quick review before App Store Connect upload.
